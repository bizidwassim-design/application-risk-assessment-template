# Application Risk Assessment Template

[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-blue.svg)](LICENSE)

A free, open-source **Likelihood × Impact (5×5)** workbook to assess information-security risks of
business-critical applications — HRIS, ERP, CRM, customer portals, AI/ML engines and more.

Aligned with **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** (clause 6.1.2), **NIST CSF 2.0** and **ISO/IEC 42001:2023**.
No macros, no add-ins, no personal data. All sample entries are fictional.

## What is inside

`Application-Risk-Assessment-Template.xlsx` — six sheets:

| Sheet | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| `01_Methodology` | Method, formula, rating bands, step-by-step instructions, standards alignment |
| `02_Scales` | Likelihood (1–5) and Impact (1–5) scales with examples, plus risk-acceptance criteria |
| `03_Application_Inventory` | Application inventory with criticality, data classification, hosting, internet exposure, AI/ML flag |
| `04_Risk_Register` | Main sheet: inherent and residual scoring, treatment, controls, mapping, owners, dates (rows 5–50) |
| `05_Dashboard` | Automatic counts by rating (inherent vs residual), KPIs, chart, prioritisation guidance |
| `06_Risk_Matrix` | 5×5 heat map, legend, and live counts of risks per cell |

## Method in one minute

```
Risk Score = Likelihood (1–5) × Impact (1–5)        → 1 to 25
```

| Rating | Score | Typical response |
|--------|-------|------------------|
| 🟢 Low | 1–4 | Accept and monitor; review yearly |
| 🟡 Medium | 5–9 | Mitigate, or accept with documented justification; review every 6 months |
| 🟠 High | 10–16 | Treatment plan within 90 days; senior-management acceptance only |
| 🔴 Critical | 17–25 | Escalate immediately; interim controls now; plan within 30 days |

Every risk is scored twice: **inherent** (before treatment) and **residual** (after existing and planned controls).

## Quick start

1. Download `Application-Risk-Assessment-Template.xlsx` and open it in Excel, LibreOffice or Google Sheets.
2. Read `01_Methodology`, then calibrate the money/downtime thresholds in `02_Scales` to your organisation.
3. List your applications in `03_Application_Inventory`.
4. Add risks in `04_Risk_Register` — pick the Application ID from the drop-down and the name fills in automatically.
5. Review `05_Dashboard` and `06_Risk_Matrix` for your management reporting.

**Conventions:** blue text = input · black on grey = formula (don't overwrite) · red target date = overdue ·
red residual score = higher than inherent (check your scoring).

## Standards alignment

| Standard | Where the template helps |
|----------|--------------------------|
| **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** | Cl. 6.1.2 risk assessment (criteria, identification, analysis, evaluation, owners) · Cl. 6.1.3 risk treatment and Annex A mapping (input to the Statement of Applicability) · Cl. 8.2/8.3 · Cl. 9.3 management review (Dashboard) |
| **NIST CSF 2.0** | GV.RM (strategy, scales, acceptance) · ID.AM (inventory) · ID.RA (register) · control mapping to PR/DE/RS/RC categories |
| **ISO/IEC 42001:2023** | Cl. 6.1.2 AI risk assessment · 6.1.3 AI risk treatment · 6.1.4 AI system impact assessment · `Contains AI/ML?` flag · Annex A references in *Control Mapping* |

A deeper cross-reference and a control-mapping cheat sheet are in [`docs/standards-mapping.md`](docs/standards-mapping.md).

## Customising

* **Scales and thresholds** — edit the tables in `02_Scales`.
* **Rating bands** — built into the rating formulas (columns K and R of the register, the Dashboard, and the legend of the matrix). Change them consistently if you need different bands.
* **Drop-down lists** — Data → Data Validation on the relevant columns.
* **More rows** — the register and inventory are pre-formatted to row 50 and 34. To extend, copy the last row down and widen the ranges used by the Dashboard formulas.

### Rebuilding the workbook from source

The `.xlsx` is generated by a commented Python script, so every change is reviewable in Git:

```bash
pip install openpyxl
python scripts/build_template.py
```

## Compatibility

* Verified by opening and fully recalculating in **Microsoft Excel (16.0)** — zero formula errors.
* Uses only standard functions (`IF`, `AND`, `ISNUMBER`, `INDEX/MATCH`, `IFERROR`, `COUNTIF(S)`, `SUM`, `AVERAGE`, `MAX`, `TODAY`), standard data validation and conditional formatting — no macros — so it is designed to work in LibreOffice Calc and Google Sheets as well.
* Google Sheets may display the chart slightly differently.

## Disclaimer

This template supports, but does not replace, professional judgement, legal advice or a certification audit.
Scores are only as good as the evidence behind them. Use roles (not personal names) and keep client names,
credentials and confidential incident details out of the file.
